## Hi, I'm Dylan!

I'm currently a Data Analytics Consultant at the Information Lab.

I graduated from Lehigh University in December 2025 with a Bachelor of Science in Business Analytics with a minor in Computer Science. 

At Lehigh, I developed a strong foundation in data analysis, visualization, and programming through projects using Tableau, SQL, Python, R, and Java, while also building business knowledge in finance, accounting, and marketing.

Before joining The Information Lab, I worked as a Procurement Analyst at Madison Square Garden, where I analyzed procurement data, built reports and visualizations, and presented insights to senior leadership. These experiences made me realize my passion for transforming complex data into clear, actionable insights that help people and positively impact decision-making.

Outside of work, you’ll find me playing piano, exploring music theory, staying active in the gym, cooking, and expanding my creative and personal growth.
